Submission

Submission and publication information will be confirmed shortly.

Submission Quidelines

U-SPACE seeks original research and practical experience papers. A good submission should make the problem, system boundary and evidence easy to understand. It should also explain what remains uncertain.

  • State the research question or operational problem.
  • Describe the operating context, assumptions and dependencies.
  • Report validation conditions, limitations, failure modes and uncertainty.
  • Discuss human roles and accountability where the system affects safety or rights.
  • Address relevant security, privacy, ethical, health and environmental concerns.
  • Explain how the work could inform further research, testing, standards, policy or operation.

The U-SPACE Workshop invites submissions in the following categories:

  1. Regular papers (up to 10 pages, excluding references), presenting original research on theories, techniques, systems, and tools for designing, validating, operating, and evaluating dependable, secure, and resilient systems for urban air mobility, U-space, and related domains.
  2. Practical experience reports (up to 10 pages, excluding references), describing the application of methods, tools, and techniques in real-world or challenging operational settings. Submissions may report on lessons learned, deployment experiences, and implementation challenges.
  3. Industry papers (up to 10 pages, excluding references), addressing industrial use cases, practical applicability, scalability, emerging technological challenges, and the deployment of U-space and urban air mobility systems. These papers should emphasize practical insights and industrial relevance.
  4. Short papers (up to 6 pages, including references), presenting preliminary research, position papers, emerging ideas, or descriptions of tools, prototypes, and operational system designs.

Paper Length and Proceedings

Authors may choose to have their accepted workshop papers considered for publication in the Springer companion proceedings of EDCC 2027. Authors considering this option should note that Springer generally prefers papers of at least 12 pages in total. Therefore, submissions in the first three categories should aim for at least 10 pages of main content, plus references. Short papers may be up to 6 pages, including references, and may consequently be less suitable for inclusion in the Springer proceedings.

References are excluded from the page limits for regular papers, practical experience reports, and industry papers. Subject to the applicable proceedings requirements, authors of accepted papers may be allowed one additional page, excluding references, to address reviewers' comments.

All submissions must be written in English and follow the Springer single-column proceedings format on A4 paper. Pages must be numbered. Templates and further information are available in the Springer instructions for authors and editors.

Review Process

All submissions will be evaluated through a peer-review process conducted by the U-Space Workshop Program Committee, comprising experts in the relevant research and application domains. Each submission will receive three independent reviews. Papers will be assessed based on their originality, significance, technical correctness, relevance to the workshop, clarity of presentation, and potential impact on research and practice.

Based on the reviewers' assessments, the Program Committee will make the final decisions on acceptance. Authors will be notified of the outcome and may be asked to address reviewers' comments in a revised version of their paper. Subject to the applicable proceedings requirements, accepted papers may be allowed one additional page, excluding references, to accommodate revisions requested during the review process.

Publication and Presentation

Authors of accepted papers may choose to have their contributions considered for publication in the Springer companion proceedings of EDCC 2027, subject to the applicable publication requirements. Authors should ensure that their manuscripts comply with the relevant formatting and page-length guidelines. Submission to the workshop does not, by itself, guarantee publication in the companion proceedings.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the main conference and present the work at the workshop. Remote presentations may be considered in exceptional circumstances, but the presenting author must still register for the conference. Further information about registration is available on the conference registration page.
